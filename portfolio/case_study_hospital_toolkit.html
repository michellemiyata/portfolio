My Hospital Toolkit

An accessible patient information booklet designed in both physical print and digital formats to make hospital visits calmer and easier to navigate.

Process

User Research & Surveys

Format

Physical & Digital Booklet

Accessible Communication

About the Project

Developed as a group project, My Hospital Toolkit helps patients prepare for appointments, find their way through hospital zones, and communicate clearly with staff. I conducted the user research and worked with my team to run surveys understanding what makes hospital visits stressful or confusing for patients.

Based on our survey findings, we designed an 8-page physical booklet ("No Phone Needed") paired with a QR-linked digital toolkit featuring audio read-aloud for Talk Cards, adjustable large text, and high-contrast mode.
